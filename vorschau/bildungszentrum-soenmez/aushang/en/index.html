Frequently asked questions

Code 95 in Dortmund, in short.

When are the courses?
On Saturdays, as a full week, or by arrangement. For companies, we set the dates so that your fleet keeps running.
What does it cost?
€120 per module for Code 95 periodic training. €2,500 for the accelerated initial qualification. €250 for the forklift licence. Certificate, lunch and course materials are included.
Which courses do you offer?
Code 95 periodic training, accelerated initial qualification, forklift, loader crane, excavator and specialist seminars for haulage operators and transport managers.
What time does it start and end?
08:00 to 16:00, one module per day. The office is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 17:00.

When does your Code 95 expire?

Enter the date. You will see at once how much time is left and when you should start.

The date is on your Driver Qualification Card (FQN). On older driving licences it is in the line with the number 95.
