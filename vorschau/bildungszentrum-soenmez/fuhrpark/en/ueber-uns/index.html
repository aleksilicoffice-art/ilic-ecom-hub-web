The owner

Zülküf Sönmez

Zülküf Sönmez founded the training centre in 2016. Since then, over 5,000 participants have been trained, and around 480 companies send their drivers to him.

What matters to him: experienced instructors, teaching in a language the driver really understands, and a course day on which nobody has to bring anything. Food, drinks, course materials and parking are provided.

Instructors and languages

Teaching in six languages.

Several experienced, multilingual instructors. They respond to the drivers' questions, so that everyone understands the content and does not just sit through the day.

Approval and supervision

Checked and approved by the Bezirksregierung Arnsberg.

Bildungszentrum Sönmez is approved as a training centre for the accelerated initial qualification and the periodic training of professional drivers, under Section 9 of the German Professional Drivers' Qualification Act (BKrFQG). Every course comes with a certificate that shows the content, date and duration.
