For hauliers and fleets

Your haulage company sends one list. Your drivers keep driving instead of chasing expiry dates.

Around 200 companies send their drivers to Wickede. This is how a company registration works:

  1. 1 Send your driver list Name, expiry date and missing modules for each driver. By email, WhatsApp or phone. The button above opens an email with a ready-made table header.
  2. 2 Dates by arrangement We spread your drivers across the Saturdays before their expiry dates. If you wish, as one full week or as a closed group for your company, up to 25 participants.
  3. 3 One invoice to the company One invoice for all your drivers instead of separate receipts. No application to any authority, no waiting for approval.

    Expiry

    When does your Code 95 expire?

    Month and year are enough. The expiry date of your Code 95 is on your Driver Qualification Card, or on older driving licences in the line with the number 95. You will see at once how much time is left and when you should start.

    Every five years, 35 hours of periodic training are due: five modules, each on one Saturday.

    We count from the first day of the month, so you are on the safe side.

    Frequently asked questions

    What drivers and transport planners ask us about Code 95.

    In what order do the five modules run?

    In a cycle on Saturdays: one to five, then from the start again, one module every Saturday. The timetable shows which module runs on which Saturday. We tell you the best Saturday to start when you sign up.

    What does Code 95 cost, and what is included?

    €120 per module, €600 for all five. Certificate, lunch, drinks, course materials and parking are included. Payment is by invoice or cash on site. We do not take card payments.

    Can my employer register me?

    Yes. Many drivers come through their company. The employer registers you and receives the invoice. You receive the certificate.

    My German is not good. Will I understand the classes?

    We teach in German, Turkish, English, Russian, Arabic and in Croatian, Serbian or Bosnian. Tell us which language you need when you sign up.

    We have twelve drivers with twelve expiry dates. How does that work?

    You send us the list with names, expiry dates and missing modules. We spread the drivers across the Saturdays, or if you wish as a closed group or as one full week. You receive one invoice for all your drivers.

    How many participants are in one course?

    Up to 25. That is the legal maximum for periodic training under the BKrFQG.

    I need the lorry driving licence. Do you do that?

    No, we do not offer the category C or CE licence ourselves. We work with a driving school and refer you to them. The accelerated initial qualification for entering the profession is done here at our centre.
